# 📚 Hungarize Comprehension Exercises

Welcome! This repository contains comprehension exercises designed for **Hungarize Daily Dose of Hungarian members**. 

## What is Hungarize? 🇭🇺

Hungarize is an innovative Hungarian language learning program created by Barbi and Laci, certified teachers of Hungarian as a Foreign Language. We believe that anyone can learn Hungarian with the right tools and genuine support.

### Our Approach

We don't do boring drills or endless memorisation. Instead, we focus on:
- **Regular, meaningful input** through realistic dialogues and authentic Hungarian conversations
- **Practical resources** you can actually use and enjoy
- **Autonomous learning** that fits into your daily routine (even just 15 minutes a day!)
- **A supportive community** of learners from around the world

Our **Daily Dose of Hungarian** program delivers daily, independent dialogues with audio, transcripts, translations, word lists, grammar notes, and practice tasks—all structured to help you build confidence in listening, reading, speaking, and writing.

## 📖 About This Repository

This repo contains comprehension exercises and learning materials exclusively for our members. These exercises are designed to complement your work in the Daily Dose of Hungarian program and help you consolidate what you're learning.

**This repository is for Hungarize members only.** If you're not yet a member, we'd love to welcome you! 💛
